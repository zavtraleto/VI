## Title

VI

## Description

```text
VI CORE 2.06. Previous start: H13.03.21. Link device: NOT FOUND. The program runs in emulation mode.

VI is the operating program of a link table, written at a Japanese institute for sessions with the other side. The table, a board of dice, is not connected. The program still starts, emulates the table, and waits for a subject. Subject file No.07: UNREGISTERED.

Six channels, one for each face of a die. You stand on a die and roll it across the board. Bring together as many equal faces as the face has pips - two 2s, three 3s, six 6s - and the group goes under the surface: sent. While it is leaving, the channel is open: add to it, and the signal multiplies. What comes up from below is noise. When noise fills the board, the link is lost.

PROTOCOL - a session without a limit.
LIMITED - the session of the day: three minutes, dealt the same to every subject, one table of results for the day.
EXERCISE - calibration of the six channels.
TASKS - thirty boards to clear in the fewest moves.

After a session something may come through. The program does not say what it is.

Dice action puzzle. Keyboard and touch.
```

## How to play

```text
OPERATING PROCEDURE

1. Move. Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD. Touch screen: swipe up, down, left or right; hold to repeat. The die under you rolls one cell and a side face comes on top. The seal in the corner shows the faces of your die.
2. Group. A face with N pips needs N dice showing it, side by side: two 2s, three 3s. The group sinks and scores its face times its dice.
3. Chain. While a group is sinking, bring one more die with the same face next to it. Every link multiplies the score.
4. One. A 1 never forms a group. Bring a 1 next to a sinking group and every other 1 leaves. One stays: the one you stand on.
5. Floor. When your die is gone you stand on the floor. From the floor dice are pushed. Step onto a die that is coming up while it is still low.
6. End. Dice keep coming up. A full board ends the session; in LIMITED the session also ends after three minutes.

TASKS are not timed: a step onto the next die is free, a roll into an empty cell is a move. Clear every die in the fewest moves. Z takes a move back, R starts the task again.

Enter - confirm. Esc - pause. SYSTEM - sound, motion, on-screen buttons instead of swipes.
```
