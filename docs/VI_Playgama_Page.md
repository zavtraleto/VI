## Description

```text
An old program from a Japanese institute, found and running again. It was written for sessions with the other side. Its table of dice is gone, so the program emulates it and waits for someone to lead the dice.

Dice that sink below the surface are sent across. Sometimes, after a session, a message comes through.

Roll the dice, gather equal faces into combos and clear the board. 100 levels and an endless mode.
```

## How to play

```text
Gather combos: as many dice side by side as the number on their top face. Two 2s, three 3s, six 6s. A combo leaves the board.

Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to roll. Z to undo, R to restart, Esc to pause.
Mouse or touch: swipe, or tap where to go.
```
